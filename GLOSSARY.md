# Second Brain

A personal system for managing life's actions and responsibilities.

## Language

**Action item**:
An independently completable question or statement, optionally accompanied by supporting bullets.
_Avoid_: Task (when referring to an action item)

**Action step**:
A bullet belonging to an action item that expresses a step or nuance, including a question or statement. Its wording and association with its parent action item are preserved.

**Action list**:
The collection of action items and their completion states.

**Source event**:
A Google Calendar event selected for extracting action items from its title or details.

**Source record**:
A record in an input source from which action items are extracted. A source event is the Google Calendar form of a source record.

**Source-written date stamp**:
The original creation timestamp of a source record, retained unchanged after edits and distinct from import time, modification time, or a scheduled event date.

**Action store**:
The authoritative destination holding action items and their user-managed workflow state, initially Linear and replaceable later.

**Import identity**:
The identity used to recognize an action previously imported from a particular source record or recurring series. It is independent of action wording and the destination issue identifier.
